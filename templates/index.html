RESEARCH PROTOTYPE - STAGES 1-4

Explainable diabetes risk Digital Twin

Select a numbered patient from the active dataset, inspect the model explanation, and compare a manual what-if scenario.

For research and demonstration only. This is not a medical diagnosis, treatment recommendation, or causal forecast.

HOW TO USE THIS SYSTEM

Follow these steps in order

  1. Open the dashboard.{% if hosted_mode %}Use this hosted Vercel page; no local Docker process is required for the hosted demonstration.{% else %}Keep Docker Desktop running, then open http://127.0.0.1:5000.{% endif %}
  2. Select a dataset patient.Enter a patient number and choose Load patient, or choose Use in the numbered table.
  3. Wait for the patient analysis.The first run can take one or two minutes while the prediction model loads and the matching 3D body is generated.
  4. Read Stage 1.Review the predicted risk category and the three model probabilities. These are research-model outputs, not a diagnosis.
  5. Read Stage 2.Review the strongest SHAP factors. They explain what influenced this prediction but do not prove medical causes.
  6. Inspect the Stage 3 twin.BMI changes body shape, sex selects the SMPL model, and predicted high-risk probability changes colour. Drag to rotate and scroll to zoom.
  7. Explore Stage 4.Connect all 21 observations with their decoded states, SHAP contributions, prediction probabilities, model evaluation, current Digital Twin, and optional local-model guidance.
  8. Try a what-if scenario.Edit the allowed values and choose Compare scenario. The current and scenario results and 3D twins appear side by side.
  9. Repeat with another patient.Select a new number to update all four stages and regenerate the 3D twin for that current patient.

Optional: Import a compatible CSV below to test another patient dataset. The imported data stays active until the app restarts.
